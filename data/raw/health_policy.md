## 1. Eligibility Requirements
Health Plus Premium is available to all primary policyholders between the ages of 18 and 65 at the time of enrollment. Dependent coverage extends to spouses and unmarried dependent children up to age 26. Pre-existing conditions are covered after a standard waiting period of 90 continuous enrollment days, provided there has been continuous active coverage.

## 2. In-Network vs. Out-of-Network Benefits
- **In-Network Care:** The policy covers 90% of all eligible medical expenses after the annual deductible is met. The annual individual in-network deductible is $500, and the family deductible is $1,000. Co-pay for standard in-network primary care physician (PCP) visits is fixed at $20 per visit.
- **Out-of-Network Care:** Emergency services are reimbursed at in-network rates. Non-emergency out-of-network services require a 40% co-insurance by the member after an individual out-of-network deductible of $1,500.

## 3. Prescription Drug Formulary & Tiers
- **Tier 1 (Generic Medications):** $10 co-payment per 30-day supply.
- **Tier 2 (Preferred Brand-Name Drugs):** $35 co-payment per 30-day supply.
- **Tier 3 (Non-Preferred Specialty Drugs):** 20% co-insurance up to a maximum cap of $150 per prescription. Prior authorization is mandatory for specialty biologic treatments.

## 4. Exclusions and Limitations
The Health Plus Premium plan does not cover elective cosmetic surgeries, routine adult dental whitening, non-prescribed alternative holistic therapies (such as aromatherapy or crystal therapy), or experimental medical treatments not approved by the regulatory health board.
